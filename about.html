01The Practice · 2003 — 2026

I came up through film and media, so I never learned to stay in one lane.

Most teams staff this range across three or four hires: product, systems, brand, art direction, motion, and sound. I carry it as one person. On a team, that means I plug in wherever the work needs me and can speak every specialist’s language while I’m there. AI is in the workflow, not in the credits. I use Claude Code for working prototypes and production front-end, Figma Make to take a direction to a clickable prototype in an afternoon, and agent workflows for the multi-step passes like audits, variant production, and documentation. That buys back the hours that go into finish. Over the past year this has turned into AI-assisted design engineering. I design and build in one pass and take an idea to production-grade front-end instead of stopping at comps. This site and my Morning Intel dashboard were both built that way.
