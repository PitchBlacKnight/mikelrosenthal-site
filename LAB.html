03The workflow
i.

Browse — scroll this page or the source sites above. Every demo here runs on your site's exact stack, in your palette.

ii.

Copy — hit the Copy button on any cell. The snippet is self-contained: markup + style + script. Paste it into any page and it works.

iii.

Adapt with Claude — found something elsewhere? Drop the URL or a screenshot into a Claude session with: "adapt this into my site's style — see LAB.html for my stack." New experiments get added here first, then promoted to the live site.

iv.

Requirements — GSAP + ScrollTrigger load in this page's <head> via CDN (both free). Snippets marked "No library" or "Pure CSS" work anywhere, even in emails-adjacent contexts.
